## 1. PREAMBLE & APPLICABILITY

Proxima ("Platform", "Service", "we", "us", or "our"), engineered by Yugam ("Yugam HQ"), is a private, multi-tenant geospatial networking and chapter intelligence platform developed exclusively for inducted business chapter members, regional affiliates, and executive leaders.

This Privacy Policy constitutes a legally binding document between you ("Data Principal", "Member", "User") and Yugam as the **Data Fiduciary**. It sets out the basis on which your personal, commercial, and geographical data is collected, processed, presented, and secured within Proxima instances.

## 2. STATUTORY COMPLIANCE: DPDP ACT 2023

In strict compliance with India's **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (Act No. 22 of 2023)** and corresponding state digital security guidelines:

1. **Notice & Consent (Sections 5 & 6):** Personal and business profile data is published solely on the basis of explicit, affirmative consent provided upon membership induction or administrative onboarding into an authorized chapter.
2. **Purpose Limitation (Section 6(1)):** Data is processed strictly for specified, lawful business networking purposes and is never repurposed.
3. **Data Minimization (Section 8(1)):** We collect only the minimum required information necessary to locate commercial premises and facilitate peer-to-peer 1-to-1 business connections.
4. **Data Accuracy (Section 8(2)):** Mechanisms are provided to ensure member listings, office locations, and communication coordinates remain accurate, verified, and up-to-date.
5. **Security Safeguards (Section 8(4)):** Industry-standard technical, organizational, and physical encryption safeguards are instituted to prevent unauthorized access, data breach, or leakage.
6. **Data Principal Rights (Sections 11–14):** Rights to access, correction, erasure (right to be forgotten), and grievance redressal are strictly honored.

## 3. NATURE OF DATA COLLECTED & PROCESSED

Proxima does **NOT** collect intrusive personal data, financial payment credentials, health telemetry, or real-time 24/7 background location tracking. We only process:

### A. Business Profile Data (Provided by Member or Chapter Secretariat)
- **Full Legal & Professional Name:** Identity verification of the inducted representative.
- **Company / Enterprise Name:** Registered legal or trade entity.
- **Business Category / Classification:** Industry classification (e.g., Corporate Law, Architecture, HVAC Engineering).
- **Executive Profile Photograph / Avatar:** High-resolution professional headshot for spatial identification on the map pins.
- **Business Coordinates (Latitude & Longitude):** Verified geographic latitude and longitude coordinates corresponding exclusively to approved **commercial offices, factories, retail outlets, or registered corporate headquarters**.
- **Commercial Street Address & Locality:** Physical business address for driving navigation.
- **Direct Business Contact Coordinates:** Official business WhatsApp number, mobile telephone, and corporate website.
- **1-to-1 Meeting Availability Status:** Toggle status indicating readiness to receive peer members.

### B. Ephemeral Client-Side Geolocation (Optional "Near Me" GPS Radar)
- If you voluntarily click the **"Near Me"** or **"Detect Location"** GPS trigger on the platform, your browser's native HTML5 Geolocation API requests temporary coordinate access.
- **Zero Server Storage:** Your live GPS coordinates are processed **entirely client-side in browser RAM** to calculate distance radii (e.g., 3km, 5km, 10km) relative to static chapter offices. **We do not store, track, log, or broadcast your personal GPS movement.**

### C. Technical & Session Data
- **No Third-Party Cookies:** Proxima operates completely free of advertising cookies, pixel beacons, canvas fingerprinters, or behavioural ad trackers.
- **Cryptographic Chapter Access Tokens:** Access links utilize high-entropy, unguessable cryptographic tokens (e.g., `proxima.yugamhq.com/?c=token`) to isolate chapter tenants without requiring third-party authentication surveillance.

## 4. PURPOSE LIMITATION & SPECIFIC USAGE

Your data is processed strictly for the following enumerated purposes:
1. Facilitating verified intra-chapter peer referrals and one-to-one executive business meetings.
2. Rendering visual spatial pins to assist traveling chapter members in visiting fellow members' commercial premises.
3. Providing driving directions and routing via external mapping providers (Google Maps, Apple Maps, OpenStreetMap) at the member's initiation.
4. Chapter administration and verifying membership active standing.

**We never sell, lease, monetize, broker, or exchange member data with advertisers, third-party marketing firms, or data aggregators.**

---

## 5. ZERO CROSS-CHAPTER EXPOSURE (STRICT TENANT ISOLATION)

Each Yugam chapter operating on Proxima is cryptographically siloed.
- A member of **Chapter Alpha** cannot view, search, or extract data from **Chapter Beta** unless granted authorized multi-chapter governance credentials.
- Public search engines (Googlebot, Bingbot, etc.) are explicitly barred via `robots.txt` and meta headers from indexing chapter directories.
- Chapter instances are accessible only via unguessable chapter tokens distributed by Chapter Directors.

## 6. RIGHTS OF THE DATA PRINCIPAL (SECTIONS 11–14 OF DPDP ACT 2023)

As a verified Data Principal, you possess statutory rights enforceable at any time:

### A. Right to Access Information (Section 11)
You have the right to receive a summary of your personal and commercial data published on the chapter directory and verify all pin coordinates.

### B. Right to Correction and Completion (Section 12)
If your commercial address, phone number, category, or photo changes, you may submit an immediate rectification request through the **Admin Portal** or by notifying your Chapter Admin. Corrections will be reflected immediately.

### C. Right to Erasure / Right to be Forgotten (Section 12(3))
If you resign, complete your chapter tenure, or choose to withdraw publishing consent:
- Your listing and map coordinates will be permanently purged from the active chapter directory within **24 hours** of administrative notification.
- Non-renewed or resigned members are automatically hidden in real-time.

### D. Right of Grievance Redressal (Section 13)
You have the right to lodge a formal grievance with the Data Fiduciary regarding any aspect of data processing.
